COMPLEXITY ANALYSIS

1. MAX HEAP

Heap height:
For 7 elements,

Height = floor(log2(7)) = 2

Insertion:
O(log n)

Access highest priority:
O(1)

Deletion of highest priority:
O(log n)


2. HEAP SORT

Best Case: O(n log n)
Average Case: O(n log n)
Worst Case: O(n log n)

Extra Space: O(1)


3. QUICK SORT

Best Case: O(n log n)
Average Case: O(n log n)
Worst Case: O(n^2)

Extra Space:
O(log n) average case due to recursion.
